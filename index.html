Reading notes. Platform figures are from the snapshot of 18 Aug 2026. Tenants reheat 18 & 30 months and Landlords Reheat 8 Months had no metrics captured at snapshot time (shown as — and drawn hollow on the timeline). In the lead-type chart, shared-audience campaigns (Strala; Octopus Renovation Offer) have their volumes split evenly between audiences. Legacy rows are earlier journey versions (2025 launches) whose message metrics predate the platform snapshot; their deals and revenue are real and included.

Revenue methodology. Revenue is the company’s gross commission (AED) on all deals — closed and in progress, cancellations excluded — belonging to leads each campaign contacted. Deals were matched two ways: CRM campaign tags, and reconciling the outreach platform’s send log against CRM lead IDs (99.9% of contacted leads matched). Refer campaigns (any Octopus, Strala, Lomond, Open Hub or “Refer” journey) are excluded from all deal and revenue figures — they route interest to partner offers, so conversions are recorded outside this system; deals from leads a refer campaign shared with a Retain or Reheat journey are credited to that journey. Deduplicated programme total: AED 2,531,486 over 114 deals. A deal is credited to every campaign that touched the lead, so campaign rows overlap and their sum (≈AED 2.97m) exceeds the deduplicated total; two proven cross-source duplicates were removed from the headline.

The platform’s “open rate” equals clicks ÷ messages.
